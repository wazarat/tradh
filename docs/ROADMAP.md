# Roadmap

**Launch — Build and Trade** (In development): Create investment strategies with AI, explore supported markets, and manage trades manually or through controlled automation.

**Next — Test and Optimize** (Planned): Run historical backtests, stress-test investment strategies, optimize risk controls, and access expanded trading infrastructure.

**Later — Verify and Discover** (Planned): Introduce ERC-8004 agent identities, verifiable strategy track records, reputation systems, and the ability to discover and follow other strategies.
